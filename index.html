How it works

Start playing in three easy steps.

1

Choose players

Select Me vs Bot to play O as the computer, then enter your X name. Select Two Players to enter both the X and O player names.

2

Set the challenge

Choose Easy for a random bot or Strong for perfect classic 3×3 play. Then choose your level: 3×3 needs 3 in a row, 4×4 needs 4, and 5×5 needs 5.

3

Start and take turns

Press Start game, then choose an empty square. X always starts. Against the bot, wait for its O move before selecting your next square.
